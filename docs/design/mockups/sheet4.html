Round 4 · A calendar in My plans, a calendar date picker, and Add to calendar

My plans gets a List / Calendar switch: a month grid with a dot per plan (green going, amber waiting, coral offer, violet hosting), and tapping a day lists its meetups, with overlaps flagged. When you host, the start date is picked on the same grid showing your own plans, then a time. Confirmed guests and hosts get “Add to calendar”: a .ics download, or a Google Calendar link.
